# Social Content Calendar — Xpenz 30-Day Launch

**Platforms:** Instagram (primary), TikTok (reels), LinkedIn (carousel + native posts), X/Twitter (low-effort), Facebook (cross-post).

**Posting cadence:** 3× per week minimum. Tuesday + Thursday + Saturday is a solid backbone. Reels Tuesday, carousel/static Thursday, story takeover or behind-the-scenes Saturday.

**Voice:** one independent talking to another. Self-deprecating, specific, never preachy. Show real screenshots, real workspaces (kitchen tables, coffee shops, home offices, job sites), real receipts (blurred amounts if private).

## 5 short-form reel scripts (15–30 seconds each)

### Reel 1 — "Receipt avalanche"

**Hook (0–2s):** *Camera shows hands opening a bag or junk drawer. Receipts fall out.*
**On-screen text:** "POV: it's August. You haven't done taxes yet."

**Cut (2–8s):** *Sweeping up receipts, throwing them on a table.*
**Voiceover:** "Every year. The same pile. The same April panic. The same CPA bill that doubles because they have to sort it."

**Cut (8–15s):** *Phone in hand, snapping a receipt with Xpenz. UI overlay shows the AI extraction.*
**Voiceover:** "Or — you do this. 3 seconds. Vendor, amount, project, client, tax category. Done."

**End card (15–18s):** "xpenz.us — 30 days free."

---

### Reel 2 — "How I tag every client expense in 3 seconds"

**Hook (0–3s):** *Quick close-up of you walking out of a coffee shop or office supply store, latte / supplies in hand.*

**Cut (3–12s):** *Toss the receipt on the table. Pull out phone. Open Xpenz. Snap. AI fills in fields. You tap the client name. Tap the payment method. Done.*
**Voiceover:** "Snap. Pick the client. Pick how I paid. That's it. Six seconds. Now that $48 latte-and-laptop session shows up on the right project at the right amount when January hits."

**End card (12–15s):** "xpenz.us — 30 days free, no card."
