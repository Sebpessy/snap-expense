# Video Script — "Home Depot Receipt → Done in 3 seconds"

The hero product demo. One actor (the business owner), one Home Depot receipt, one phone, three versions of the same story tuned for different placements.

**Audience:** any small-business owner who pays for materials at retail — contractors, remodelers, handymen, landlords, flippers, sole proprietors. (Home Depot is the venue; the message generalizes to any business making expense purchases.)

**Voice:** dry, confident, slightly amused. No marketing-speak. Builder-meets-Stripe.

---

## Production parameters

| | Reel (TikTok / IG / paid social) | Web Hero |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16 vertical | 16:9 horizontal |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 @ 30fps | 1920×1080 @ 30fps |
| Music | Upbeat indie / chill electronic, ~110-120 bpm, royalty-free (Epidemic Sound: "Lemon Juice" or similar) | Soft synth pad, no melody, ambient |
| Voiceover | First-person, casual, ~140 wpm | Same |
| Color grade | Warm, slightly contrast-y, like the existing hero photo | Same |
| Captions | Burned-in (TikTok / IG default) | Off (web has its own UI text) |

**Locations:**
- Home Depot exterior (or any generic home-improvement store — if Home Depot doesn't give release rights, blur the logo or shoot a generic supply store)
- Pickup truck cab OR workshop bench OR home office desk (one location, your choice)

**Props:** real Home Depot receipt with several line items (lumber, drywall screws, drill bits, painters tape, etc.), iPhone with Xpenz pre-installed and logged in, work gloves on the dashboard for texture.

**Talent:** one person, 30-50yo, dressed in real work clothes (Carhartt jacket, henley, jeans). Hands visible (the audience needs to feel like it's their own hands). Face optional — POV shots work great.

## Version C — 60-second landing-page hero video

Slower, more cinematic. For embed on the marketing site or a YouTube pre-roll.

### Beat sheet

1. **0:00–0:08 — Cold open**
   - Aerial drone shot over a residential street at golden hour. Pickup truck pulls into a driveway with materials in the bed.
   - VO: *"Every business has the same problem with receipts. You buy something. You forget what for. Tax season hits."*
   - Quick montage: shoebox, glovebox, junk-drawer, email-inbox-full-of-receipts.

2. **0:08–0:16 — The before-state**
   - Cut: kitchen table at night. Hands sorting paper receipts under a single lamp. A calendar shows JAN 28.
   - VO: *"Every January, the same fire drill. Every April, the same CPA bill — doubled because they had to sort it for you."*
   - Text overlay: **"There's a better way."**

3. **0:16–0:24 — Hero moment: the snap**
   - Cut to daytime. Walking out of a Home Depot. Receipt in one hand, phone in the other.
   - In the truck cab. Receipt on the dashboard.
   - Open Xpenz. Camera viewfinder. Tap.
   - VO: *"Snap a receipt with Xpenz. That's it."*

4. **0:24–0:38 — The AI does the work**
   - Tight, screen-recording quality close-up of the phone. Four green detection boxes draw onto the receipt one by one:
     - **VENDOR · Home Depot**
     - **TOTAL · $487.23**
     - **DATE · May 22**
     - **CARD · VISA ••••1009**
   - Each box accompanies a small chime + sparkle.
   - Four extraction rows below fade in with green checkmarks.
   - Auto-category: "Materials & Supplies · Schedule C · Line 22".
   - VO: *"AI reads the vendor, the total, the date — and even the last four of your card. Everything's already filled in. You just tap to assign it to a project, and tap save. Three seconds, done."*

5. **0:38–0:48 — The compound result**
   - Cut to clean Xpenz dashboard on the phone. Four projects with budget bars. Color-coded category breakdown.
   - VO: *"Now every expense is tagged to a project, a client, and a tax category. You can see which work is making you money, in real time."*
   - Cut to: laptop CSV export landing on a CPA's desk.
   - VO: *"At month-end, hand your CPA one clean CSV. Done."*

6. **0:48–0:54 — Social proof / breadth**
   - Quick split-screen montage: contractor in a truck → freelance photographer at a coffee shop → e-commerce seller at a kitchen island → landlord at a job site.
   - Each one snaps a receipt. Each one's app shows their own clean dashboard.
   - VO: *"Whatever your business looks like, Xpenz fits it."*

7. **0:54–1:00 — The offer + CTA**
   - Closing card: Xpenz wordmark, big amber "**30 DAYS FREE · NO CREDIT CARD**", URL `xpenz.us`.
   - VO: *"Xpenz. 30 days free at xpenz.us."*
   - Hold for 2s on the URL.

## Filming checklist

**Before the shoot:**
- [ ] Pre-load Xpenz on the phone with realistic-looking demo data (4-5 active projects, recent expenses, no real PII).
- [ ] Get the receipt. Real Home Depot receipt. Make sure the total is clearly readable (something like $487.23 — round-ish but specific).
- [ ] Charge the phone. Disable notifications. Set Do Not Disturb.
- [ ] Wipe the phone screen clean.
- [ ] Test the Xpenz camera flow once to make sure all 4 detection boxes appear cleanly.
- [ ] Scout the Home Depot exterior — find an angle that's clearly "home improvement store" without showing the actual Home Depot logo unless you've cleared rights. Safer alternative: any generic big-box hardware store (use "Building Supply Co." dressing in post if needed, or shoot the door from an angle that hides signage).

**During the shoot:**
- [ ] Tripod for the screen-recording close-ups — phone in a stable position, perfect lighting, no glare on the screen.
- [ ] Multiple takes of the snap moment (it's the hero shot — needs to be perfect).
- [ ] B-roll: lots of it. Receipts, materials, truck dashboard, calendar pages, kitchen-table moments.
- [ ] Talent's face is OPTIONAL — POV / over-the-shoulder / hands-only works great and broadens relatability.

**Post:**
- [ ] Music license cleared.
- [ ] Burn captions for vertical reels (TikTok / IG default).
- [ ] Audio: voiceover recorded clean, no room reverb. Music ducked under VO by 8-10dB during dialog.
- [ ] Export 3 cuts: 15s, 30s, 60s. Same footage, different rhythm.
- [ ] Add UTM-tagged URL to the end card description (not on-screen — keep the visual clean).

## Alternative receipt scenarios (same script, different store)

If Home Depot doesn't fit your target customer or you want to test variants:

| Receipt vendor | Audience signal |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Home Depot / Lowe's** | Trades, contractors, landlords, flippers |
| **Office Depot / Staples** | Freelancers, consultants, agencies, small office |
| **Costco / Sam's Club** | Restaurants, e-commerce sellers, larger small business |
| **Gas station (Shell, Chevron)** | Universal — every business has vehicle expenses |
| **Adobe / SaaS receipt** | Creatives, designers, dev-shops |

Same shot list, swap the vendor. A/B test which audience responds best.
